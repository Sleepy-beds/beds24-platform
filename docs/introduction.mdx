**Sleepy-beds** makes [Beds24](https://beds24.com) easy to use from a modern stack. It's a
small, dependency-free **core SDK** with everything else — payments, email, LINE
notifications, framework helpers — layered cleanly on top.

## Who it's for

- Developers integrating Beds24 into **Next.js / React / WordPress** sites
- **Small inns, guesthouses, and minpaku** who want booking pings in their **LINE** group
- Anyone automating reservations, availability sync, or webhook workflows

Built and dogfooded by an actual guesthouse operator. Everything is **MIT licensed**.
